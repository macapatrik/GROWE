Enquiries and agreeing a project

The website form is for non-binding enquiries. Submitting it does not create an order with an obligation to pay or a contract for a project. We agree the specific scope, price, tax treatment, timing and other cooperation terms before entering into a contract.

Prices and times shown on the website are indicative for the described scope. Binding terms for your project are confirmed individually.

Questions and complaints

Send project questions, feedback or complaints to info@growe.cz or to the registered office. Include the project identification and a description of the issue. This does not affect your statutory rights or agreed contractual terms.

Out-of-court consumer dispute resolution

If you are a consumer and cannot resolve a service-contract dispute directly with us, you may contact the Czech Trade Inspection Authority, the competent body for out-of-court consumer dispute resolution. Details and how to submit a proposal are available at coi.gov.cz/informace-o-adr. This procedure covers consumer disputes, not disputes between businesses.
