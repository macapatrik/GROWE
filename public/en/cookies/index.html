What this website uses

The current version of growe.cz does not set its own cookies or use localStorage or sessionStorage. It does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or other advertising or analytics trackers. Images and fonts load from our hosting; pages have no embedded third-party videos, maps or social widgets.

We therefore do not display a cookie banner or request consent for analytics or marketing cookies. If we introduce such tools, we will update this information and let you decide before activating tools that require consent.

What stays in your browser

Your browser may keep website files such as images, styles and fonts in its normal cache to speed up loading. This supports technical page delivery and is not a visitor-tracking identifier. Animation controls and open menus work in the memory of the current page; we do not create a persistent visitor profile.

The form does not send information as you type. It sends it to our server only when you press “Send enquiry”. Any autofill or saved contact details are controlled by your browser and its settings.

Spam protection and hosting logs

The form uses a hidden protective field and a server-side rate limit. It does not use an external CAPTCHA or store a cookie on your device for this protection.

The hosting service processes technical data to deliver pages and may maintain operational logs. Their scope, purpose and your rights are explained in the privacy policy. A server log is not a browser cookie.

Links to other websites

Portfolio examples may link to client websites. Those sites load only after you open the link and have their own privacy and cookie policies.

Questions and browser settings

You can manage cookies and cached files in your browser settings. Contact info@growe.cz with questions. The operator is Patrik Máca, Business ID 17738750; full contact details appear in the legal notice.
