What we process and why

Enquiries and preparation of a project. We process your name, email, message and any optional phone number, selected service, budget or deadline you provide. We receive these details directly from you and use them to reply, prepare a proposal and discuss your project. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. If you are a company contact or send a general question, the basis is our legitimate interest in business communication under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Your name, valid email and message are needed to submit the form. Your phone number is optional. Providing data is voluntary, but without contact details we cannot reply. Please do not include sensitive data or passwords. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a newsletter.

Security and website operation. The hosting service processes technical details, particularly your IP address, request time, requested address and browser information, to deliver pages, diagnose errors and prevent abuse. The basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, functional website under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

The form also uses a short-term one-way hash of your IP address and the request time to limit repeated submissions. This protective record does not contain your enquiry.

How long we keep data

Enquiries that do not lead to a project: no more than 12 months after the last communication. We then delete them unless specific correspondence is needed as evidence or to protect rights in an actual dispute.

Agreed projects: during the cooperation, followed by retention only of data needed for legal obligations or the protection of rights. The period depends on the document type, statutory retention period or applicable limitation period; for disputes it also depends on their resolution. You receive further project-specific information when agreeing the project.

Form protection: the IP hash and time are used in a 60-second window; older entries are removed when another request arrives. This is not a visitor database. Hosting logs: retention follows the provider’s configuration and retention rules for security, diagnostics and incident handling. A specific incident record may be retained as needed to resolve the incident and protect rights. Details about the retention of particular data are available on request.

Who may access data

Your enquiry is delivered to info@growe.cz. The controller and people assisting with enquiries or technical operations may access data only as necessary.

Hosting, the info@growe.cz email inbox and server-side email sending are provided by INTERNET CZ, a.s. (FORPSI), Business ID 26043319, Ktiš 2, 384 03 Ktiš, Czech Republic. We may disclose data to public authorities where required by law, or to advisers when protecting rights.

The website has no third-party advertising or analytics tools. The enquiry form does not involve intentional transfers outside the EU/EEA or to international organisations. If an additional service is used for a particular project, we explain its processing terms separately.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you may request access and a copy of your data, correction, erasure or restriction of processing. For processing based on a contract, you may have a right to data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation.

Write to info@growe.cz. We respond without undue delay, normally within one month. Where justified, this may be extended by a further two months; we explain the reason and extension in time. We may reasonably verify your identity to protect your data.

You may complain to the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, Czech Republic. Consent is not requested for the purposes above. If we request consent for a different purpose, it will be separate and you will be able to withdraw it.

Cookies and automated decisions

We do not carry out profiling or decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing. The form’s technical rate limit only helps prevent spam.
